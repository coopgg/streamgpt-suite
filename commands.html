🤖 AI Chat Powered by CoopGPT
!coopgpt
Learn what CoopGPT can do and get a quick rundown of active commands.
!shoutout @user Mod
Generates an AI-powered shoutout for a streamer. Pulls their info and writes something fresh every time. Mod and broadcaster only.
ex!shoutout @SomeStreamer
!lurk
Let chat know you're stepping away but still supporting the stream. The lurk is acknowledged. You are seen.
🎮 Chat Games Win Coop Coins
!triviaCoins
Starts an AI-generated trivia question. First person to type the correct answer in chat wins coins. You have 30 seconds.
!riddleCoins
Starts an AI riddle. Think it through — first correct answer wins coins. You have 60 seconds.
!madlibsCoins
Starts a Mad Libs game. Chat fills in words one at a time — noun, verb, adjective, celebrity, place, silly sound. The AI weaves them into a story and reads it to chat. Everyone who contributes earns coins.
!20qCoins
Starts a round of 20 Questions. The AI picks something — a person, place, or thing. Chat asks yes/no questions to narrow it down. First person to guess correctly wins coins.
!capitalsCoins
Starts a US state capitals quiz. The bot names a state — first to type the correct capital wins coins.
!cyoa
Starts a Choose Your Own Adventure. The AI writes a story and chat votes on what happens next. The story evolves based on whatever the Coop Troop decides.
!8ballquestion
Consult the magic 8-ball. It knows things. It does not explain itself.
ex!8ball Will I hit Diamond this season?
!quickmathCoins
A math problem drops in chat. Type only the number — fastest correct answer wins coins.
!chuck
Delivers a random Chuck Norris fact. Every single one is true.
!dadjoke
Get a dad joke. It will be terrible. You asked for this.
!funfact
Get a random fun fact. Educational content. Technically.
!darkjoke
Get a dark joke. These go places. Viewer discretion advised.
🏛️ Destiny Active during Destiny 2 streams
!towerthoughtDestiny
Summons a Destiny-themed shower thought from the Tower. The kind that makes you stare at the ceiling for five minutes thinking about Ghosts and the Vex.
!rallyflagDestiny
Rally to the flag. You know what to do.
Destiny streams only. These commands are active when CoopGG is live on Destiny 2.

💰 Coins & Economy Earn and spend Coop Coins
!earnCoins
Learn how to earn Coop Coins — by watching, winning chat games, and participating in stream events.
!balance
Check your current Coop Coin balance. Know your numbers.
!give@useramount
Give some of your Coop Coins to another viewer. Generosity is valid.
ex!give @CoopGG__ 100
!gambleamount
Gamble some of your Coop Coins. Double up or lose it all — that's the game.
ex!gamble 200
!heistamount
Start or join a coin heist. Put some coins in, and if the heist succeeds, everyone walks away with more. If it fails — well.
ex!heist 500
!leaderboard
See the top Coop Coin holders. Clout, measured in fake money.
Coop Coins are earned automatically while watching and by winning chat games. The more you participate, the more you earn.
🎲 Events Raffles, giveaways & viewer queue
!raffle
Enter the current raffle when one is running. CoopGG or a mod will announce when a raffle is open and what the prize is. One entry per viewer.
!giveaway
Enter the current giveaway when one is active. Watch for CoopGG's announcement in chat — giveaways are announced with instructions before entries open.
!join
Add yourself to the viewer play queue for Playing With Viewers sessions. CoopGG will announce when the queue is open and what game is being played.
Raffle, giveaway, and viewer play sessions are announced live in chat. Watch for the announcement before using these commands.
💬 Quotes Stream memory
!quote
Pulls a random saved quote from the stream archive. Things were said. They have been preserved.
!addquote text Mod
Saves a quote to the archive. Mod only — so the archive doesn't immediately become a chaos document.
ex!addquote "I did not expect that" - CoopGG, 2024
ℹ️ Stream Info Quick lookups
!uptime
See how long the stream has been live. Useful for knowing how deep into the session we already are.
!discord
Get the link to the Coop Troop Discord. Come hang out between streams.
!rules
Get the stream rules. Short version: be a normal person and everyone has a good time.
!deaths
See the current death count for the session. Sometimes the number is very high. We do not talk about it.
!dead Mod
Increment the death counter by one. Mod only, because CoopGG will not be counting his own deaths.
⭐ VIP Channel Point Redemption
Timed VIPChannel Points
Redeem channel points to receive VIP status for a set number of days. VIP is automatically granted when you redeem and automatically removed when time is up. Subscribers get an extended duration.
This is a Channel Point Redemption, not a chat command. Find it in the channel points menu below the stream.
