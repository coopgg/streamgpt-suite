Command reference

Chat commands

Every chat command that ships with StreamGPT Suite, organized by category. Brackets mean optional.

AI and Chat Fun Chat Games Coins Pokémon Community Moderators

AI and Chat Fun

Powered by your bot
!botname
A short tour of the bot: games, coins and social commands. The command matches the bot name you set in config.py.
@botname[message]
Mention the bot in chat and it replies to you. It answers to the bot name you set in config.py.
!lurk or !brb
Let chat know you are away. The bot sends you off in style.
!8ball[question]
Ask the magic 8 ball.
!towerthought
A funny shower thought written by the AI.
!roast[@user]
A playful roast. Leave the name off and a random viewer gets it.
!ship@user[@user]
A friendship compatibility score for two chatters. The same pair always gets the same score.
!fortune
Your fortune-cookie fortune, with a lucky number.
!cookie or !slap or !hug or !fart or !corner or !ban
Aim it at a chatter with an @name, or leave it off for a random one. The ban is fake. Nobody is actually banned.
!HBD[@user] or !HappyBirthday
Wish a happy birthday to someone.
!dadjoke
A random dad joke.
!funfact
A random fun fact.

Chat Games

Win coins
!trivia
Multiple choice trivia. Answer A, B, C or D within 30 seconds.
!riddle
A riddle to solve. Close answers count.
!quickmath
Solve the math problem before time runs out.
!capitals
US state capitals quiz. Answer A, B, C or D.
!madlib
Everyone adds words to build a Mad Lib. Contributors earn coins.
!cyoa
Choose Your Own Adventure. Vote 1, 2 or 3 to steer the story.
!20q
20 Questions: the bot picks something and chat asks yes or no questions.
!ask[question] or !guess[answer]
How you play 20 Questions: ask a question, or make a guess.
!end20qMod
End a 20 Questions game early.

Coins

Name set in config.py
!balance
Your coin balance.
!leaderboard
The top coin holders.
!earnCoins
Every way to earn coins.
!daily
Claim your daily coins. Claiming on back-to-back days builds a streak bonus.
!give@user[amount]
Send some of your coins to another viewer.
!gamble[amount]
Spin the slot machine with a bet. Minimum 10 coins.
!heist[amount]
Start a crew heist. Others have 60 seconds to join with !heistjoin. By default half of heists succeed and pay double, and the AI writes the story.
!heistjoin[amount]
Join a heist that is recruiting. Minimum bet 100.
!duel@user[amount]
Challenge someone for coins. They answer with !duel accept or !duel decline.
!bet[option][amount]
Bet on a running prediction.
!predict
See the running prediction. Mods start, lock and pay out predictions.
!addCoins@user[amount] or !setCoins@user[amount]Mod
Add to, or set, a viewer's balance.
!resetAllCoinsStreamer
Reset every balance to zero.
!rank[@user]
A one-line profile: coin rank, Pokédex count and daily streak.

Pokémon

Catch them all
!pokemon
A wild Pokémon appears. Everyone gets a short window to throw a ball.
!pokeball[type]
Throw a ball. Name a bought ball type for better odds.
!pokedex[@user]
Your catch count. Add top for the leaderboard.
!pokebox[@user]
The Pokémon you own. Add dupes to see spares.
!shop or !buyball[type][amount] or !balls
See ball prices, buy balls with coins, and check how many you have.
!trade@user[yours]for[theirs]
Offer a swap. They answer with !accept or !decline.
!accept or !decline
Answer a trade offer.
!partner[name]
Pick a Pokémon you own to show next to your name.
!sell[name] or !release[name]
Sell spare copies for coins. You always keep one of each, and shinies are never sold.
!pokeevent
Is a special event, like Shiny Hour, running? Mods can start or stop one.

Community

Together
!boss
Check the boss: health, time left and top damage dealers. Mods start the fight.
!attack
Hit the boss. It costs coins, and everyone who swung shares the loot if it goes down.
!addme or !removeme
Join or leave the queue to play with the streamer.
!queue or !showplayers
See who is in the queue and who is playing.
!enter
Enter the giveaway.
!enterraffle
Enter the raffle.
!birthday[month/day]
Save your birthday, show it, or remove it. Month and day only. Get a greeting and a coin gift on the day.
!vipDays
How many days of timed VIP you have left.
!watchtime[@user]
How long you have watched while the stream was live, and where you rank among viewers. Counting began when the feature was turned on.

Moderators

Mods and broadcaster only
!so@userMod
Shout out another streamer with an AI-written message that mentions their game.
!permit@user[seconds]Mod
Let someone post links for a short time.
!strikes[@user]Mod
See who has chat strikes and what their next one costs.
!pardon@user[all]Mod
Remove a strike after a mistake, or all of them.
!updatetitleMod
Change the stream title or game, or have the bot write a fresh title.
!startgiveaway[prize] or !pickwinner or !clearentries or !giveawaystatusMod
Run a giveaway.
!startraffle or !drawraffle or !clearraffle or !rafflestatusMod
Run a raffle.
!addplayer or !nextplayer or !randomplayer or !removeactive or !replaceactive or !clearplayersMod
Manage the play-with-viewers queue.
!predictstart or !predictlock or !predictwin or !predictcancelMod
Run a coin prediction.
!bossstart[minutes] or !bossstopMod
Start or call off a boss fight.
!addDays@user[n] or !takeVIP@user or !logVIPsMod
Manage timed VIPs.
!setbirthday@user or !clearbirthday@user or !birthdaysMod
Set, clear or list birthdays.
!testalertStreamer
Test an alert on stream.

Commands that use the bot name follow the name you set in config.py. Reward amounts, timers and the currency name are settings too, so your numbers may differ from the defaults shown here.
